## Keyword research (WebSearch, 2026-10-11)
- Pay-per-reply / done-for-you agencies: market is hybrid (flat tech fee plus per-positive-reply fee) or flat retainers of roughly $2,000-6,000/mo. Pure "infra at cost, fee only on reply" is a real differentiator. Vendor roundups are self-ranking, so trust is a gap for newcomers.
- Deliverability: search intent clusters on SPF/DKIM/DMARC, Google/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (spam complaints under 0.3%), domain warmup 4-6 weeks, separate sending domains. Matches existing /outreach tips and the deliverability service page.
- Benchmarks: "cold email reply rate benchmark 2026" is crowded; the most cited average is ~3.4% (Instantly), sources range 0.45% to 10%+ depending on method. Benchmark Checker should state its method and denominator to be citable.
- South Africa: few local managed agencies surfaced (DevCommX Johannesburg, FirstSales landing page). Low local competition, supports a "cold email agency South Africa" angle.
- Target map (unchanged from strategy): home "managed cold email outbound"; services "done for you cold email service", "email deliverability consulting", "b2b lead generation service"; tools "cold email roi calculator", "cold email benchmarks"; outreach long-tail.
